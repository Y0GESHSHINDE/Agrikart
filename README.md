# **Agrikart - Farm Equipment Rental Service**

## **Overview**
Agrikart is an innovative platform designed to address the challenges faced by farmers in accessing and utilizing farm equipment efficiently. The app enables farmers to rent and share agricultural machinery within their local communities, reducing costs and maximizing resource utilization. With features like dynamic pricing, AI-based recommendations, and a trust-based rating system, Agrikart promotes sustainable and collaborative farming practices.

---

## **Problem Statement**
Farmers often face two major issues:
1. **High Costs**: Owning farm equipment is expensive, and many small-scale farmers cannot afford it.
2. **Underutilization**: Equipment owned by some farmers remains idle for long periods, while others struggle to access the tools they need.

Agrikart solves these problems by creating a structured platform for renting farm equipment, ensuring cost-efficiency, resource-sharing, and improved productivity.

---

## **Key Features**
1. **Equipment Listing and Booking**:
   - Farmers can list their equipment for rent.
   - Users can search, book, and schedule equipment rentals with ease.

2. **Dynamic Pricing**:
   - Rental rates adjust based on demand, season, and equipment availability.

3. **AI-Based Recommendations**:
   - Suggests the best equipment based on user preferences, location, and farming needs.

4. **Trust and Rating System**:
   - Ensures transparency and reliability through user reviews and dual ratings (for both owners and renters).

5. **Multi-Language Support**:
   - Makes the platform accessible to farmers across different regions.

6. **Maintenance Tracking**:
   - Automates equipment servicing schedules to ensure optimal performance.

7. **Delivery and Pickup Services**:
   - Offers convenient delivery and pickup options for rented equipment.

8. **Blockchain Security**:
   - Ensures secure transactions and data integrity.

---

## **How It Works**
1. **Registration**:
   - Farmers register using their mobile number or Aadhaar number.
   - Owners list their equipment with details like availability, pricing, and location.

2. **Search and Booking**:
   - Renters search for equipment, check availability, and book rentals.
   - AI-based recommendations help users find the best equipment for their needs.

3. **Payment and Scheduling**:
   - Secure payment options are provided.
   - Renters receive booking confirmations and reminders for returns.

4. **Equipment Tracking**:
   - Owners and renters can track equipment usage and maintenance schedules.

5. **Feedback and Ratings**:
   - After the rental period, both parties can leave reviews and ratings.

---

## **Technical Stack**
- **Frontend**: React Native (for cross-platform mobile app development).
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express.js (for server-side logic).
- **Database**: MongoDB (for storing user and equipment data).
- **AI/ML**: Python (for recommendation systems and dynamic pricing).
- **Blockchain**: Ethereum (for secure transactions and smart contracts).
- **Cloud Hosting**: AWS or Google Cloud (for scalability and reliability).
